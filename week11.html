JUNE 2026
Week 11
Sharpening the edges.

Week eleven is the penultimate week for the Omnichannel workstream, and a week of focused research preparation across Digital and AI. Everyone is heads down, sharpening content and designs as deliverables take their final form, prototypes go in front of users, and the work converges toward our next share.

Here is what each team got up to this week.

What we did
📍 Omnichannel Work Stream
  • With next week being the last for this workstream, the team has been fully focused on shaping the final deliverables. We completed the first version of all journey maps and shared them with the Nekua team.
  • We continued developing the Blueprint, capturing the operational layer of the experience including backstage dependencies, metrics, and the distinction between MVP and future moments.
  • We shaped the Developed Signature Moments, a playbook-style deck that brings together all the materials from this workstream and shows how they connect to help the Nekua team move forward with confidence.
Next steps
  • Refine the content across all deliverables.
  • Polish the design of all deliverables.
  • Prepare for the client share on the 6th.
📱 Digital Work Stream
  • We prepared the concepts and prototype for user testing, getting them ready to put in front of real users.
  • We met with Davide from Interbrand to align on colour and AI character for the app, ensuring the digital experience connects seamlessly with the overall brand.
  • We refined the design system and the screens for onboarding, home, protocol, and report, bringing greater consistency and polish across the product.
  • We designed the flow and screens for the profile page and the friends, family, and community section.
Next steps
  • Run user testing, synthesise the learnings, and refine the design accordingly.
  • Refine the UI and AI character.
  • Design the notification settings.
🤖 AI Work Stream
  • We created a constitution for the AI, capturing its core beliefs, ways of thinking, and approach to helping users — the character behind the companion.
  • We produced a first draft of the conversation types, defining the range of interactions the AI will be able to have.
  • We trained the prototype on the constitution and conversation types, bringing the AI companion to life for the first time.
  • We laid out a map of the system behind the AI, giving the wider team visibility into how the companion is structured and how it thinks.
Next steps
  • Set up the prototype for research and internal experiments.
  • Tweak and update the drafts based on initial feedback.
  • Explore the look and feel of the AI and what it outputs.
See you next week!
