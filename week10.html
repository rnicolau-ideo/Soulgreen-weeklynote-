Project Alchemist
JUNE 2026
Week 10
Personas, protocols, and prototypes.

Three workstreams, one coherent vision: the Omnichannel team has given shape to the people at the heart of Nekua, the Digital team is building the screens they will use, and the AI team is training the companion that will guide them.

Here is what each team got up to this week.

What we did
📍 Omnichannel Work Stream
  • We developed detailed user profiles for each persona, bringing the Nekua customer to life with clarity and depth.
  • We mapped a journey for each persona, highlighting their different entry points, interactions, motivations, and behaviours across the experience.
  • We began structuring the blueprint, which will capture the more operational aspects of the experience including backstage dependencies, metrics, and the distinction between MVP and future journey moments.
Next steps
  • Share a first draft of the journeys with the Nekua team to gather feedback and develop them together.
  • Develop the blueprint in detail.
📱 Digital Work Stream
  • We explored the UI and information architecture of the Home and Protocol screens, shaping how users will navigate their daily Nekua experience.
  • We explored options for the daily Circadian dial, a key design element that reflects the rhythm of the user's day.
  • We iterated on different AI touchpoints within the app, exploring how the companion shows up across the digital experience.
  • We created the flow for onboarding and the weekly self check-in, two of the most important moments in the user journey.
  • We created a user flow map to align the Digital workstream with parallel workstreams and ensure a coherent experience end to end.
Next steps
  • Explore further versions of the protocol and AI touchpoints.
  • Finalise the flow and document the key touchpoints to design for.
  • Create prototypes for user testing.
  • Act on feedback from the team.
  • Work with Interbrand on product colours and AI companion visualisations.
🤖 AI Work Stream
  • We created a constitution for the AI, capturing its core beliefs, ways of thinking, and approach to helping users — the character behind the companion.
  • We produced a first draft of the conversation types, defining the range of interactions the AI will be able to have.
  • We trained the prototype on the constitution and conversation types, bringing the AI companion to life for the first time.
  • We laid out a map of the system behind the AI, giving the wider team visibility into how the companion is structured and how it thinks.
Next steps
  • Set up the prototype for research and internal experiments.
  • Tweak and update the drafts based on initial feedback.
  • Explore the look and feel of the AI and what it outputs.
See you next week!
