Week 05
From many voices, one direction.

Welcome to the fifth Project Alchemist weekly note, your window into the work as it unfolds.

This week was a full one: we went deep into the field, then surfaced with a clear point of view on where we're going. Here's what we got up to, and what's coming next.

What we did
  • We completed six user interviews testing early experience visions and associated product concepts, with Nekua team members joining us as observers throughout.
  • We ran a thorough download and synthesis of the interview learnings, then hosted a session with the Nekua team to walk through what users responded to across the different directions and concepts.
  • Drawing on what tested best in research, we evolved the explored visions into a single, unified experience direction. From this, we developed a set of core design principles to guide what comes next.
  • We began bringing the evolved vision to life through new concepts and early ecosystem thinking, exploring how the experience could extend across touchpoints.
  • We prepared for next week's Learnings Share and Making Session with the Nekua team, a hybrid in-person and remote session designed to turn insight into action.
NEXT WEEK
  • We will host some of the Nekua team in our London office for a Session on Monday.
  • We will refine the final Experience Vision based on the work we do together during the session.
See you next week!
