Week 07
One chapter closes, another opens.

This week we brought the Vision workstream to a close, leaving us with a strong shared foundation to build from. But this is less an ending than a starting point: the work ahead is where the vision becomes real.

Here's what we got up to, and what's coming next.

What we did
  • We worked on onboarding materials to bring the new team members joining next week fully up to speed, ensuring everyone hits the ground running from day one.
  • We began preparing for the third round of testing, which will feed directly into the Digital Experience workstream.
  • We selected and designed the Signature Moments, defining the key experiences that will shape how people feel at every meaningful touchpoint of the Nekua journey.
  • We developed early prototypes for each Signature Moment, giving us tangible, testable expressions of the vision to carry into the next phase.
  • We prepared for and delivered the Final Vision Workstream presentation, including design principles and the early prototypes for the signature moments. We then ran a "What Would Have to Be True" session to surface the key questions and details we need to address across each Signature Moment in the weeks ahead. This was a crucial step for us to capture development needs for each signature moment going forward.
  • We held a catch-up with BIP to bring them up to speed on the work developed so far, setting the stage for alignment and collaboration in the weeks ahead.
  • We connected with the Nekua team to work through the feedback from yesterday's session. A genuinely productive conversation to align on the path forward and the type of sessions that are necessary to get input on critical decisions. With the Signature Moments now set, the new workstreams will be where we bring those moments to life in full detail.
