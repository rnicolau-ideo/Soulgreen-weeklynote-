JUNE 2026
Week 09
Deep foundations, sharper details.

Week nine is where things start to take tangible shape. Across all three workstreams, the team has been translating direction into decisions, defining the product experience, shaping the digital foundations, and mapping the intelligence behind the AI companion.

Here is what each team got up to this week.

What we did
📍 Omnichannel Work Stream
  • We defined the core product experience, clarifying what the Nekua digital app needs to be at its most essential.
  • We prioritised the Signature Moments to develop, focusing the team's energy on what will have the greatest impact.
  • We mapped the key design questions and assumptions needed to develop the customer journey.
  • We prepared for and ran an alignment session with the Nekua team to ensure we are building in the right direction.
  • We aligned with the Digital and AI workstreams on shared priorities and dependencies.
Next steps
  • Develop an initial Journey Map, including customer personas.
  • Begin developing the priority and future Signature Moments.
📱 Digital Work Stream
  • We explored the look and feel of the app, establishing the visual language that will define the Nekua digital experience.
  • We defined the experience building blocks, the core components that will be reused across the product.
  • We designed the onboarding experience, shaping how new users will first encounter Nekua.
  • We drew inspiration from a series of expert interviews, bringing external perspective into the design process.
Next steps
  • Develop Signature Moment wireframes.
  • Build a prototype for user testing.
  • Prepare the design research plan.
  • Connect with Interbrand on visual direction.
🤖 AI Work Stream
  • We explored three distinct temperaments for the AI companion, surfacing the nuance in tone of voice needed for different users and contexts.
  • We mapped the different conversation types that will occur within the app, building a clearer picture of how the companion will show up across the experience.
  • We began defining the goals, intent, and guardrails for each conversation type, the foundations of a responsible and coherent AI personality.
  • We built a high-level agent map, giving the team a shared view of how the AI system is structured.
Next steps
  • Explore the trust framework that will govern how the AI companion interacts with users.
  • Further define the purpose, inputs, and outputs for each agent in the system.
See you next week!
